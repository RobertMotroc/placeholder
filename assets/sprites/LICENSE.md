# Sprite licence

All pet sprites come from two free CraftPix packs:

| Pets | Pack |
|---|---|
| hare, fox, deer, boar, black-grouse | CraftPix free forest animals pixel art |
| bull, calf, chick, lamb, piglet, rooster, sheep, turkey | CraftPix free farm animals pixel art |

Licence: https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/ (the packs' `license.txt` contained only this link). Source: https://craftpix.net/freebies/

Read the licence before distributing the app, including whether attribution is required.

The PNGs here are the packs' "with shadow" exports. Forest sheets are copied as is; farm sheets were split into `walk.png` and `idle.png`. The editable `.aseprite` and `.psd` files are in `art-source/`.
